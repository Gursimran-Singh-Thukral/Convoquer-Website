# Wireframe Blueprint: Home / Landing Page (`/`)

## 1. Visual Mood & Atmosphere
- **Theme**: Golden Hour (Dusk Stadium Lighting & Collegiate Championship Glory)
- **Primary Base**: Night Wine (`#0D0205` / `#180308`) with Burgundy (`#800020`) atmospheric glows.
- **Accents**: Crimson Carrot (`#FF4500`) for adrenaline/live pulses and Gold (`#FFD700`) for champion highlights.
- **Editorial Anti-AI Stance**: No cookie-cutter centered marketing fluff. Uses bold athletic typography, high-contrast match tickets with perforated stubs, and live monospaced flip boards.
